# Product direction

WearCheck helps a guest prepare for an occasion: confirm the host's requirements, check the outfit they plan to wear, and compare a suitable correction on their own photograph before deciding what to wear or consider buying. Keeping an already compatible outfit is a complete outcome.

Gemini extracts visible evidence; WearCheck applies the confirmed requirements and filters references; YouCam Clothes V4 or Shoes V3 generates the visual preview. A successful generation is not a compliance verdict. The complete generated outfit needs a separate assessment, including remaining layers, shoes and uncertainty.

Current catalogue photographs are licensed visual references. They have no live stock, price, size availability, checkout, affiliate relationship or merchant endorsement. Uploaded references remain private to a check and must pass an AI suitability reading before paid generation. This reading can be wrong; the whole preview and the actual garment still need human inspection.

A future retail integration would need authorised merchant feeds, stable product and variant identifiers, current availability and price timestamps, image rights allowing virtual try-on, supported garment categories, and a clear destination for purchase. Requirement filtering would run before ranking; the selected variant's exact image would be bound to the generation request. Stock and price would be refreshed before an outbound purchase link, with referral relationships disclosed. None of that retail functionality is implemented or claimed today.

Demonstrated value is a working event-preparation workflow and visual comparison. Reduced returns, increased conversion, improved confidence and wider demand remain hypotheses requiring user research and measured trials.
